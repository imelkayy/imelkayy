Create Your Own Tattoo

Step 1: Create Your Design

  • Design Your Tattoo: Use any graphic design software (like Photoshop, GIMP, or Paint.NET) to create your tattoo design. Use this file, as a template for the tattoo's location. Remember, every tattoo works for both male and female.
  • Save Your Design: Ensure to save your image as a PNG, this allows for the best quality. Also ensure that the output image is 2048x2048, anything less and the image will lose quality.
