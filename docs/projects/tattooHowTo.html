Create Your Own Tattoo

Step 1: Create Your Design

  • Design Your Tattoo: Use any graphic design software (like Photoshop, GIMP, or Paint.NET) to create your tattoo design. Use this file as a template for the tattoo's location. Remember, every tattoo works for both male and female.
  • Save Your Design: Ensure to save your image as a PNG, this allows for the best quality. Also ensure that the output image is 2048x2048, anything less and the image will lose quality.

Step 2: Upload Your Design

  • Upload Process: Instructions on how to upload your design will be here.
    • Step 1: Upload your Tattoos to an image-hosting service, such as imgur. Please note the maximum size we accept is 2048x2048.
    • Step 2: Open a Tattoo Ticket in our discord. These tickets can be found in the #tickets channel, located under the Support Desk category. Ensure you open the correct ticket type.
    • Step 3: Fill out the form for opening the ticket. You will be asked to provide a Name for your Tattoo, as well as the URL to the hosted image.
    • Step 4: Wait for our team to review the submitted material. If we notice any issues, we will inform you inside the ticket.
  • Confirmation: Details on how you'll receive confirmation of your upload.
    • Keep an eye on your ticket. Our team will let you know if there are any issues and when the Tattoo has been submitted into the system.
