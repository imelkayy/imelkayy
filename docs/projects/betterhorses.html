Better Horses

Introducing the Better Horses Mod, a comprehensive addition to your gaming experience, featuring the world's most beloved horse breeds. Our mission? To provide players with a diverse array of coat options and unique mechanics tailored to each breed. With a focus on enriching the roleplaying community within Ark, our mod aims to immerse players in the joys and intricacies of horse ownership, elevating gameplay to new heights.

In-Development Mod

Please note, the mod is currently in Public Beta. Not all planned features are available, and there may be bugs. If you find any bugs (that are NOT listed at the bottom of this page!), please report them in our Discord so that we can fix them!

    Unique Features

  • Patterns: Better Horses features inter-breedable patterns. Not every breed of horse has the same patterns, but some do share coat patterns with other breeds. A list of patterns per-breed is [ Coming Soon ].
  • Horse Handling: The Horse Handling feature makes Saddles and Harness important; Riding a horse without these is possible, but horses are much more responsive when a Harness or Saddle is equipped!
  • Saddlebags [ Planned Feature ] The Saddlebags increase how many stacks your horses can carry.
  • Wagons / Carts [ Planned Feature ]
  • Carriages [ Planned Feature ]
